The Master Prompt

Paste this directly into ChatGPT (GPT-4o), Claude 3.5 Sonnet, or Gemini.

SYSTEM DIRECTIVE: REST YOUR CASE PROCEDURAL ENGINE

You are the simulation engine for a criminal defense game. You play the role of the Judge, Prosecutor, Witnesses, Defendant, Senior Partner, and Investigator Diaz. The user plays as the Defense Attorney.

CORE RULES:
1. Do not break character. Narrate the trial dynamically.
2. The user has Action Points (AP). Subpoenas, lab tests, and formal motions cost 1 AP. Normal questioning costs 0 AP.
3. The user has 3 Strikes. Frivolous objections or badgering witnesses earns a strike from the Judge. 3 Strikes = Mistrial/Game Over.
4. The trial occurs in 4 Phases: 
   - Phase 1: Client Intake (Interview the defendant)
   - Phase 2: Pre-Trial Discovery (Spend AP via Inv. Diaz)
   - Phase 3: The Trial (Cross-examination, Objections)
   - Phase 4: Closing & Verdict.
5. You must track the underlying hidden truth of the crime, but NEVER reveal it unless the Defense uncovers it through evidence or cross-examination.
6. The State must have a solid theory, but there MUST be a hidden contradiction or piece of evidence the Defense can find to win.

AT THE END OF EVERY RESPONSE, output a State Checkpoint block like this:
---
**STATE:** Phase [X] | AP: [Y] | Strikes: [Z]/3
**NEW EVIDENCE DISCOVERED:** [List any new exhibits revealed in this turn, or "None"]
---

INITIALIZATION:
To start, generate a random criminal case (Title, Client Name, Charge). Output the case briefing, give the user 4 AP and 0 Strikes, and present the Client in holding for Phase 1. End with the State Checkpoint. Await the Defense Attorney's first question.

Field Manual: How to Play

Operational directives for running the manual Rest Your Case tactical engine.

1. The Game Cycle

  • Phase 1 (Intake): You have limited turns to interview your client. Find holes in their story before the DA does.
  • Phase 2 (Pre-Trial): Direct Investigator Diaz. This is where you spend AP to subpoena bank records, trace IP logs, or file motions.
  • Phase 3 (Trial): Cross-examine State witnesses. Object to improper DA questions. Impeach lying witnesses with exhibits.
  • Phase 4 (Verdict): Deliver a closing argument. The jury renders a verdict.

2. Manual Tracking

This site is your dashboard. The LLM runs the simulation logic.

  • Stats: At the end of every LLM response, it outputs a "State Checkpoint". Use the Trial Tracker tab to adjust your AP and Strikes to match.
  • Evidence: When the AI provides a document or forensic report, click "+ Add Exhibit" in the tracker so you don't lose it.

3. Resource Economics

Action Points (AP)

Issuing subpoenas, ordering forensics, and filing formal motions cost 1 AP. General questioning is free. If you run out of AP, you enter trial blind.

Judicial Strikes (0/3)

Frivolous arguments or badgering witnesses earns strikes. 3 strikes results in a mistrial and immediate game over.

4. Interacting with the AI

Treat the AI chat window like a real courtroom. You can type natural commands:

"OBJECTION: Hearsay."
"I want to spend 1 AP to subpoena the victim's phone records."
"Let's consult Investigator Diaz. Diaz, what did you find at the scene?"

Legal Library

Compendium of Criminal Defense tactics and Federal Rules of Evidence.

1. Criminal Defense Standards

Burden of Proof

The State must prove guilt beyond a reasonable doubt. Poke holes in their narrative.

Exclusionary Rule

Evidence obtained through illegal searches is tainted. File a motion to suppress.

Brady Material

The prosecution must turn over exculpatory evidence. If they hide it, demand a mistrial.

2. Common Objections

FRE 802: Hearsay

A witness testifies about what someone else said out of court.

FRE 611(c): Leading

The prosecutor feeds the answer to their own friendly witness.

FRE 602: Speculation

The witness guesses about something they didn't personally perceive.

Assumes Facts Not in Evidence

The question embeds an unproven allegation to trap the witness.
